## 2. 暗示焦点句模（Implication Focus）
### 2.1 锁骨 / 颈

- `damp hair sticks to her neck; collarbones catch warm side light`  
- `an open collar frames the hollow of the throat and collarbones`  
- `a thin silver chain rests in the sternum shadow`

### 2.2 腰腹 / 腰窝

- `oversized tee ridden up, a strip of midriff and soft waist hollow visible`  
- `the shirt hem lifts as she reaches, revealing a narrow band of bare waist`  
- `side-lying, the curve from rib to hip reads under dim key`

### 2.3 大腿 / 布料勒痕

- `shorts bite lightly into the upper thigh as one knee lifts`  
- `bare thighs against rumpled sheets, hem of the sleep shorts high but still covering`  
- `knee drawn up, fabric crease at the hip drawing the eye without explicit exposure`

### 2.4 肩带 / 领口

- `a thin strap slips off one shoulder; she does not fix it`  
- `neckline dips as she leans, soft shadow at the sternum, fabric still held`  
- `hoodie half-zipped, collarbones and the top of a sports bra line readable`

### 2.5 视线 / 被观看

- `half-lidded eyes meet the camera as if she knows she is being watched`  
- `gaze holds past the lens toward an unseen presence just out of frame`  
- `she meets her own eyes in the mirror — self-aware, unhurried`  
- `tiny smirk, soft bite of the lower lip, eyes on the phone glow then flick to camera`

### 2.6 湿发 / 浴后

- `wet hair plastered in strands along neck and clavicle`  
- `post-shower flush on cheeks, dewy shoulders under steam-soft light`
